Terms of Service

Last revised: October 2026

1. Scope of the platform

WorkCred is an informational matching platform. We provide scheduling tools, two-code attendance handshakes, and append-only Work Passport records. We are not an employer, contractor, or payment intermediary.

2. No verification of identity or guarantees

We do not perform background checks or verify governmental identity documents in this version. The "History checked" status indicates solely that both parties entered valid start and finish codes during past appointments.

3. Direct cash payments

Customers pay workers directly in cash upon completion of work. WorkCred charges zero commission and does not process payments or guarantee cash amounts.

4. User conduct and contact blocking

Users must not post abusive content, bypass security controls, or publish unauthorized contact details in public bios or captions.
