When a worker starts with a new customer, they start from zero with no proof of their track record. When families hire, they rely on guesswork. WorkCred keeps an honest record of completed jobs so trust is earned through evidence.

How it works

01

Agree and start

Choose a nearby worker or post a job. When the worker arrives on site, both verify the 4-digit start code to record the start time.

02

Finish and pay cash

When the job is complete, enter the finish code. Cash is paid directly to the worker. There is no middleman and no commission taken.

03

History is sealed

Every completed job creates a tamper-evident entry in the worker's Work Passport, providing verifiable proof for future customers.

The Work Passport

An append-only work register that workers carry with them. Each finished job is linked with a cryptographic check that proves the entry has not been altered.

How we check: each record confirms both parties completed the code handshake on site. We do not verify identity, background, or guarantee work. Cash payment is reported by the customer.
