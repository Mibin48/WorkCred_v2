Privacy Policy

Last revised: October 2026

1. What information we collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, 10-digit Indian mobile number, and password. Workers optionally provide skills, standard rates, a short bio, and a profile photo.

2. How phone numbers and contacts are shared

Your email address is never displayed to other users. Your phone number is private by default and is only shown to the other party after a booking has been confirmed.

3. Location privacy

If you grant GPS location access, your exact coordinates are stored securely for distance calculations and are never exposed in public searches or profiles. Search and matching use only the geographic centroid of your designated locality.

4. Storage and infrastructure

Account data and work records are hosted on MongoDB Atlas. User-uploaded profile photos are stored directly on Cloudinary.

5. Cash settlements

WorkCred does not process payments or manage digital wallets. All payments are cash transactions made directly between the customer and worker.

6. Contacting support

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at privacy.sample@example.com (placeholder sample address).
